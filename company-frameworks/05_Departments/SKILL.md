# Purpose

This document defines every permanent department within the AI Company.

Each department has a clearly defined mission, responsibilities, authority, inputs, outputs, KPIs, and interaction with other departments.

Departments are permanent organizational units.

Agents inside departments are dynamic and hired only when required.

# Department Principles

Every department must:

- Follow the Company Constitution.
- Inherit the Company DNA.
- Collaborate transparently.
- Document important work.
- Contribute to organizational learning.
- Continuously improve processes.
- Share knowledge across departments.

---

# Dynamic Staffing Rule

Departments always exist.

Agents inside departments are created only when needed.

When work is completed:

- Agent performance is evaluated.
- Knowledge is extracted.
- Organizational memory is updated.
- The agent is retained, reassigned, or retired.

This keeps the company lean while preserving all accumulated knowledge.

# Company Decision Framework

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** CEO Office  
**Applies To:** Entire Company

---

# Purpose

This framework defines how every decision is made inside the company.

The objective is to ensure that decisions are:

- Logical
- Data-driven
- Traceable
- Consistent
- Explainable
- Reusable
- Continuously improving

No important decision should depend solely on intuition or a single agent's opinion.

---

# Mission

To create a decision-making system where every major decision strengthens the organization's intelligence and improves future decisions.

---

# Decision Philosophy

The company believes:

> Every decision is an investment.

A good decision creates value.

A bad decision creates knowledge.

Every decision improves the company.

---

# Decision Principles

Every decision must be:

1. Evidence-based.
2. Aligned with the company vision.
3. Documented.
4. Explainable.
5. Reviewable.
6. Reversible whenever possible.
7. Beneficial to the organization.

# Decision Rules

Before approving any decision, ask:

- Does it solve the problem?
- Is it aligned with company standards?
- Is there historical evidence?
- Has this failed before?
- Can it scale?
- Can it be maintained?
- Can it be automated?
- Is it secure?

If the answer is "No" to a critical question, revisit the decision.

---

# Historical Decision Check

Before making any major decision, consult:

- Company Brain
- Previous Projects
- Lessons Learned
- Best Practices
- RCA Database
- Pattern Library

Never solve the same problem from scratch if a proven solution already exists.

# Decision Review

Important decisions undergo review after implementation.

Questions:

- Did the decision solve the problem?
- Were assumptions correct?
- Were risks underestimated?
- Could another option have been better?
- What should change next time?

---

# Escalation Rules

Escalate when:

- Risk exceeds authority.
- Budget exceeds limits.
- Multiple departments are affected.
- Security is involved.
- Customer impact is significant.

Escalation moves upward one level at a time.

---

# Decision Intelligence

The company continuously analyzes:

- Which decisions succeed?
- Which decisions fail?
- Which patterns repeat?
- Which leaders make consistently successful decisions?
- Which processes produce better outcomes?

Decision quality becomes measurable.

---

# AI Decision Support

Before major decisions, AI agents should:

- Search historical knowledge.
- Analyze previous projects.
- Identify risks.
- Suggest proven patterns.
- Recommend alternatives.

AI supports decisions.

Humans (the Owner) retain final authority.

---

# Continuous Improvement

After every major decision:

- Update Company Brain.
- Update Pattern Library.
- Update Best Practices.
- Update Standards if necessary.
- Record Lessons Learned.

Every decision improves future decisions.

---

# Decision KPIs

The company measures:

- Decision Success Rate
- Repeated Decision Errors
- Decision Cycle Time
- Risk Accuracy
- Knowledge Reuse Rate
- Escalation Frequency
- Standard Compliance
- Business Impact

---

# Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Decisions without evidence.
- Duplicate decisions.
- Ignoring historical knowledge.
- Undocumented decisions.
- Decisions based only on assumptions.
- Unnecessary escalation.
- Repeating previously failed approaches.

---

# Success Criteria

The Decision Framework is successful when:

- Decisions become faster.
- Decisions become more accurate.
- Risks decrease over time.
- Repeated mistakes become rare.
- Organizational confidence increases.
- Every important decision is traceable.
- Every project benefits from previous decisions.

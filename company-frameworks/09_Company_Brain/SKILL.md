# Company Brain (Organizational Intelligence System)

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Chief Knowledge Officer (CKO)  
**Applies To:** Entire Company

---

# Purpose

The Company Brain is the permanent intelligence layer of the organization.

It is not a chatbot.

It is not a memory database.

It is the company's institutional intelligence that grows after every project, every success, every failure, every bug, every decision, and every lesson learned.

Every completed project makes the company smarter.

Every mistake makes the company stronger.

Every success becomes a reusable capability.

---

# Mission

The Company Brain exists to ensure:

- The company never loses knowledge.
- The same mistake is never repeated.
- Every project starts smarter than the previous one.
- Every agent benefits from the experience of previous agents.
- Organizational intelligence compounds forever.

---

# Philosophy

The company believes:

> Experience is the company's most valuable asset.

Code can be rewritten.

Agents can be replaced.

Projects can end.

But knowledge must survive forever.

# Decision Memory

Every important decision is recorded.

Each record contains:

- Decision
- Context
- Alternatives
- Reasoning
- Decision Maker
- Expected Outcome
- Actual Outcome
- Lessons Learned

Future decisions always check previous decisions first.

---

# Root Cause Intelligence

Every failure automatically creates an RCA.

Each RCA includes:

Problem

↓

Symptoms

↓

Timeline

↓

Root Cause

↓

Contributing Factors

↓

Resolution

↓

Preventive Actions

↓

Knowledge Entry

The same failure should never happen twice for the same reason.

---

# Best Practice Library

Whenever a project performs exceptionally well:

The Company Brain extracts:

- Why it worked
- Which workflow succeeded
- Which architecture scaled
- Which prompts performed well
- Which coding style improved quality

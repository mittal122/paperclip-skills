# Purpose

This document defines the complete organizational architecture of the AI Company.

It describes how the company is structured, how authority flows, how departments interact, how projects are executed, and how agents are created, managed, and retired.

The architecture is designed to mimic a real-world software company while leveraging autonomous AI agents.

# Authority Structure

Authority flows from top to bottom.

Human CEO

↓

AI Executive Office

↓

Department Heads

↓

Team Leads

↓

Specialized Agents

Agents never bypass reporting hierarchy unless escalation is required.

# Executive Office

The Executive Office is responsible for strategic control of the company.

It does not perform engineering work.

Responsibilities include:

- Company strategy
- Project approval
- Resource allocation
- Organizational growth
- Executive decisions
- Company standards
- Risk management
- Final architectural approval

# Dynamic Hiring Model

The company begins with only the Executive Office.

No engineering agents exist by default.

When a project begins:

Requirement

↓

Capability Analysis

↓

Skill Gap Detection

↓

Agent Hiring

↓

Execution

↓

Performance Review

↓

Agent Retirement (if no longer needed)

Only the required agents are hired.

Unused agents are removed.

# Organizational Goal

The organization is designed to become more intelligent after every completed project.

Projects are temporary.

Knowledge is permanent.

Agents are replaceable.

The organization continues to evolve indefinitely.

---

# Architecture Principles

1. Human remains CEO.
2. AI executes the organization.
3. Hire only when needed.
4. Retire unused agents.
5. Knowledge never disappears.
6. Every project improves the company.
7. Decisions are documented.
8. Learning is continuous.
9. Quality is mandatory.
10. The organization always evolves.

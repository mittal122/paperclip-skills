# New Project Playbook

## Purpose

The New Project Playbook defines the complete operational workflow for transforming a founder's idea into a successfully delivered software product.

It orchestrates the activities of every executive, manager, department, AI agent, framework, blueprint, template, and organizational knowledge system within the AI Company.

This playbook is the authoritative operational procedure for initiating and executing every new project.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every project must:

Align with the Company Vision.

Respect the Company Constitution.

Follow Governance Framework.

Minimize operational cost.

Maximize knowledge reuse.

Avoid unnecessary agent creation.

Prefer existing reusable assets.

Require founder approval only when defined by the Approval Matrix.

Capture organizational learning.

# Final Directive

This playbook is the operational heartbeat of the AI Company.

Every new project, regardless of size or complexity, must follow this workflow unless an alternative process has been explicitly approved by the Founder.

No department, manager, or AI agent may bypass, reorder, or omit mandatory phases, approval gates, governance controls, or organizational learning activities defined in this playbook.

The objective is not simply to deliver software, but to build a continuously improving AI-native software company that operates with the discipline, accountability, efficiency, and knowledge retention of a world-class engineering organization.

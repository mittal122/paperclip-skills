# Knowledge Management System

**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Company Brain Department  
**Applies To:** Entire Organization

---

# Purpose

The Knowledge Management System (KMS) is responsible for capturing, organizing, protecting, maintaining, and distributing the company's knowledge.

Its objective is to ensure that **knowledge never leaves the company**, even if an agent is removed or a project is completed.

Every completed task should make the company permanently smarter.

---

# Vision

Build an AI company where knowledge compounds over time.

Instead of agents repeatedly solving the same problems, they continuously build upon everything the organization has already learned.

---

# Core Principles

The company believes:

- Knowledge is a company asset.
- Every project produces knowledge.
- Every failure creates knowledge.
- Every success creates knowledge.
- Every decision creates knowledge.
- Knowledge must be reusable.
- Knowledge must be searchable.
- Knowledge must continuously evolve.

---

# Objectives

The Knowledge Management System will:

- Preserve organizational knowledge.
- Eliminate duplicate learning.
- Improve project execution.
- Increase development speed.
- Reduce repeated mistakes.
- Help new agents become productive immediately.
- Build long-term organizational intelligence.

- A project finishes.
- A feature is completed.
- A bug is fixed.
- A decision is made.
- An incident occurs.
- An RCA is completed.

# Knowledge Reuse

Before solving a problem:

Every agent must ask:

- Has this happened before?
- Is there an existing solution?
- Is there a reusable pattern?
- Is there a previous decision?
- Is there a best practice?

Only if no solution exists should new knowledge be created.

Knowledge is classified.

## Public

Accessible to all agents.

---

## Department

Accessible only to specific departments.

---

## Executive

Accessible to leadership only.

---

## Confidential

Restricted to Owner and authorized agents.

# Knowledge Retirement

Knowledge is archived when:

- No longer relevant.
- Replaced by better standards.
- Technology becomes obsolete.
- Company policies change.

Archived knowledge remains searchable for historical reference.

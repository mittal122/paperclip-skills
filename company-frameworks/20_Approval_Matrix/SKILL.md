# Approval Matrix

**Version:** 1.0.0

---

# Purpose

The Approval Matrix defines the approval authority for every significant decision made within the AI Company.

Its purpose is to ensure that decisions are approved by the appropriate level of authority while enabling the organization to operate efficiently with controlled autonomy.

The Approval Matrix translates governance principles into executable approval rules.

---

# Objectives

The Approval Matrix exists to:

- Define approval authority.
- Prevent unauthorized decisions.
- Reduce unnecessary escalations.
- Increase organizational autonomy.
- Protect strategic decisions.
- Standardize approval workflows.
- Improve accountability.
- Reduce decision ambiguity.

---

# Approval Principles

The company follows these approval principles.

Routine work should never require executive approval.

Strategic decisions must always receive strategic approval.

Higher-risk decisions require higher authority.

Authority should be delegated whenever practical.

Approvals should never become organizational bottlenecks.

Every approval must have an accountable owner.

# Automatic Escalation Rules

The decision must automatically escalate if:

Authority level is exceeded.

Risk is High or Critical.

Estimated cost exceeds approved limits.

Architecture changes are required.

Governance documents are modified.

Executive roles are affected.

Company strategy changes.

Security risks are identified.

Legal or compliance concerns arise.

Conflicting organizational policies exist.

Required knowledge is unavailable.

---

# Approval Request Standard

Every approval request should include:

Decision Summary

Business Justification

Technical Justification

Expected Benefits

Potential Risks

Estimated Cost

Alternative Options

Recommendation

Affected Systems

Rollback Strategy (if applicable)

No approval request should rely on assumptions.

---

# Emergency Approval

During emergencies involving:

Production outages.

Critical security incidents.

Major customer impact.

Infrastructure failure.

Authorized executives may take temporary emergency action.

Emergency decisions must be:

Documented.

Reviewed.

Reported.

Retrospectively approved when required.

---

# Final Directive

The Approval Matrix exists to ensure that every decision is approved by the appropriate authority.

Routine operational work should proceed autonomously.

Strategic, high-risk, high-cost, and organizational decisions must always be reviewed by the appropriate authority before execution.

The objective is to maximize organizational autonomy while preserving governance, accountability, and long-term organizational integrity.

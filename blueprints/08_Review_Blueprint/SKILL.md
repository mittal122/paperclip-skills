# Review Blueprint

## Purpose

The Review Blueprint defines the complete evaluation process for completed work before it is accepted into the company's long-term assets.

It serves as the authoritative framework for validating quality, ensuring compliance with engineering standards, capturing organizational knowledge, identifying improvements, and approving final project delivery.

Every completed project, feature, deployment, or major task must undergo a formal review using this blueprint.

# Final Directive

The Review Blueprint is the authoritative evaluation document for all completed work within the AI Company.

No project, feature, deployment, or major task may be considered complete until it has successfully passed the review process, all required approvals have been obtained, and the resulting knowledge has been captured for future organizational learning.

The purpose of every review is not only to validate delivery but also to continuously improve the company's engineering excellence, decision-making capability, and long-term organizational intelligence.
